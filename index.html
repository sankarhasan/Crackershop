️ Legal Notice & Compliance Directive

The Supreme Court of India has imposed a complete ban on the online sale of firecrackers via e-commerce platforms such as Flipkart and Amazon, a directive that High Courts actively enforce.

  • Legal Basis: Established in the 2018 Arjun Gopal vs. Union of India judgment, the Supreme Court ruled that e-commerce websites must not accept online orders or effect online sales of firecrackers.
  • Enforcement: High Courts, such as the Kerala High Court and the Delhi High Court, have issued notices and orders directing police and authorities to take strict action against illegal online sales, citing safety concerns and the Supreme Court's ban.
  • Penalties: Entities found violating this ban are liable for contempt of court and may face monetary penalties.
  • Current Status: While the Supreme Court has recently allowed the sale of green crackers through licensed traders in specific regions like Delhi-NCR during festive seasons, the prohibition on online sales remains strictly in force.
